          Nifty 50 Index Fund

          What is an index fund?

          Instead of betting on one company, an index fund holds small pieces of many. The Nifty 50 covers 50 of India’s largest listed companies, so your money is spread across all of them.

          You don’t pick stocks or time the market. When the index rises, your units rise with it, and when it falls, they fall too.

          Why RupeeRound uses it

          In India you can’t buy a small slice of a single stock, but you can buy index fund units in small amounts. That is why your ₹100 can go straight in.
